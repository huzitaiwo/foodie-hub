Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I track my order?

After you check out, open the tracking page. It shows each stage from accepted to delivered, an estimated time and the route your delivery partner takes.

What if my basket has items from another restaurant?

One order covers one kitchen. If you add from a different one, we’ll ask whether to keep your current basket or start a new one.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Yes. At checkout choose a time slot and the kitchen will have it ready then.

Is my card information stored?

No. This demo never saves card details. A live version would hand payment to a certified payment provider.

How do restaurants and delivery partners join?

Each has its own workspace. Open it from the links above, sign in and set up your menu, hours or availability.
